Chrometana is a powerful extension that enables users to redirect searches conducted by Microsoft's Cortana assistant to Google Chrome. With this official Chrometana free download, you can take full control of your search experience on Windows. Enjoy a complete package with all features included and stay updated with the latest improvements.

This extension allows you to centralize your online activity in Chrome, ensuring that all your browsing history is managed in your preferred browser.
